Dear Hiring Team,

I am a UX Designer and Researcher with a multidisciplinary background in education, translation, academic research and software development. I design digital products by combining user research, systems thinking and frontend implementation, translating complex ideas into clear, thoughtful and user-centered experiences.

My path into UX design developed naturally from a long-standing interest in people, learning and the way information shapes decisions. My academic background strengthened my skills in research, analysis and communicating complex findings clearly, while professional translation taught me to pay close attention to language, meaning and context.

Through developing A Terapija, a digital wellbeing platform for a real practice owner, I have worked across the complete product lifecycle. I led stakeholder discovery, clarified user needs, developed personas and user journeys, structured the information architecture, designed responsive interfaces and created a reusable design system.

I also implemented the application using Django, HTML, CSS and JavaScript. Working from early conversations to a functioning product taught me how closely research, design and implementation depend on one another. It also strengthened my sense of ownership, responsibility and ability to move a complex project forward independently.

I am particularly interested in roles where research is connected directly to product decisions and where designers collaborate closely with stakeholders, developers and other disciplines. I bring curiosity, analytical thinking, systems awareness and a strong commitment to creating digital experiences that are useful, accessible and carefully considered.

I would welcome the opportunity to contribute my research, product thinking and design skills while continuing to grow within an experienced and collaborative team.

Thank you for your consideration.

Sincerely,
Ana Masiliūnienė
