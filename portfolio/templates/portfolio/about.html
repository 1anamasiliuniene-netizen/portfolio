About

Designing calm digital experiences through research, systems thinking and frontend craftsmanship

I design products that help people feel confident rather than overwhelmed. By combining UX research, interaction design and frontend implementation, I enjoy taking ideas from early discovery to polished, working experiences.

My background in education and professional translation taught me how people learn, interpret information and create meaning. Clarity is never accidental—it emerges through research, thoughtful design and careful refinement.

My journey

Curiosity became a path

I have always been curious about how people think, learn and make decisions. Looking back, my career wasn’t a series of changes but a continuous journey. Every chapter added another perspective—from understanding people and communicating clearly to designing and building products that solve meaningful problems.

  1. 01

    2019 · Vilnius University · Master’s degree

    Educology — Education Policy

    • Human-centred thinking
    • Neurodiversity
    • Research foundations
    • Critical analysis
    • Systems thinking
    Expand

    Educology taught me that people don’t fail because they are incapable—they often struggle because systems are designed for only one way of thinking. Exploring different learning styles and neurodivergent perspectives strengthened my belief that good design should adapt to people, not expect people to adapt to the design.

    That research mindset still shapes how I conduct interviews, analyse findings and make product decisions. It reminds me to stay curious, question assumptions and validate ideas before drawing conclusions.

Principles

What guides my work

01

Clarity

I remove unnecessary complexity before adding new features. Good design helps people understand what matters and move forward with confidence.

02

Empathy

Understanding people comes before designing solutions. Research helps reveal the real problem beneath the initial request.

03

Craftsmanship

Typography, spacing and interaction details shape trust. I care about how an experience feels as much as how it functions.

04

Systems thinking

I design reusable systems rather than isolated screens, helping products stay coherent as they evolve.
